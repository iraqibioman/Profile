About

Information is only worth something when someone can act on it.

For nine years I led a four-person analytical unit at the United Nations Assistance Mission for Iraq. We produced the situational reporting mission leadership relied on — gathering evidence from the field, finding the pattern in it, and putting it in front of decision-makers before the moment passed.

Before UNAMI I ran communications for a USAID justice programme, supported a national industrial zones project at UNIDO, and led outreach for two nationwide advocacy and peacebuilding programmes at Mercy Corps. That range taught me how differently a ministry, a donor and a local NGO each read the same set of facts.

I am PMP certified, hold a master's in international business administration, and work daily in Power BI, Qlik, ArcGIS and Excel. Every annual performance review from 2014 to 2025 was rated Exceeds Expectations.
